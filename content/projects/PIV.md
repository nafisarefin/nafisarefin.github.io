---
title: "Thrombosis Risk in Aneurysmal Arteries via PIV–PLIF"
weight: 50
summary: "Experimental PIV/PLIF study of flow patterns and thrombus formation in aneurysmal arteries."
---

This experimental study investigates how blood-flow patterns inside aneurysmal arteries promote thrombus (clot) formation and growth. Aneurysms disrupt normal flow, creating recirculation and stagnation zones where clots are prone to develop, and understanding this coupling is important for assessing rupture and occlusion risk.

Flow fields were measured using Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) and combined with Planar Laser-Induced Fluorescence (PLIF) to visualize transport and mixing simultaneously. Patient-representative aneurysm models were imaged in a controlled flow loop, allowing quantitative characterization of velocity fields, shear environments, and the flow structures associated with thrombus initiation.

The project demonstrates proficiency in experimental flow diagnostics (PIV/PLIF), optical setup and calibration, phantom fabrication, and quantitative flow-field analysis. Linking measured hemodynamics to observed thrombus behavior provides experimental grounding for computational models of aneurysm thrombosis.
