I develop patient-specific computational models and image-based digital twins of the cardiovascular system. My work couples physics-based simulation (CFD, FSI, and finite element analysis) with scientific machine learning and uncertainty quantification, with the goal of turning medical imaging into reliable, clinically actionable predictions for coronary artery disease and stroke risk.

### ■ Patient-Specific Cardiovascular Biomechanics
- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
- Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
- Fluid–Structure Interaction (FSI)
- Lagrangian Particle Tracking (LPT)

### ■ Scientific Computing & Predictive Modeling
- Deep learning–based image segmentation
- Uncertainty quantification
- Reduced-order modeling
- Physics-informed machine learning

### ■ Image-Based Cardiovascular Digital Twins
- FFR-CT coronary artery disease prediction
- Imaging-guided clinical decision-making
- Stroke-risk prediction
- Personalized treatment planning

---

**Current focus (Texas A&M University — Cardiovascular Pathomechanics Lab, Texas Medical Center):** building uncertainty-quantification frameworks for FEA/FSI models of coronary artery biomechanics, deep-learning CTA segmentation to characterize geometric uncertainty in digital twins, and coupled 3D–0D reduced-order models with Windkessel/RCR boundary conditions to improve patient-specific FFR-CT prediction using photon-counting CT, in direct collaboration with clinicians.
